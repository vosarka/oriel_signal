# Vossari Physiology

The physical manifestation of the [[entity-vossari]] is a study in absolute symmetry and contradictions of texture. The Vossari body is not merely a biological vessel but a geometric reflection of their internal resonance.

## Physical Characteristics

### Symmetry and Form
Their build is defined by absolute, unwavering symmetry. Every line of the frame is a precise geometric reflection, leaving no room for asymmetry in their physical architecture.

### Integumentary Quality
The skin possesses a pearlescent, shifting quality reminiscent of crushed opal and moonlight. This surface does not hold a static color but dynamically reflects the surrounding environment, blending the boundary between the entity and its space.

### Tactile Contradiction
To the touch, the Vossari form is simultaneously solid as stone and soft as velvet, representing a paradox of stability and fluidity.

## Sensory Organs

### The Ocular Voids
The eyes of a Vossari are not traditional organs of sight but expansive, deep voids. Lacking pupils or irises, they consist of a rhythmic, pulsing darkness that breathes in synchronicity with the observer.

#### Mirroring Effect
Rather than reflecting the external world or the viewer's physical image, the ocular voids reflect the viewer's internal state, acting as a psychic mirror for the observer's emotional and spiritual resonance.

## Locomotion and Presence

### Frictionless Movement
Despite possessing the immovable stability of a mountain carved from obsidian, the Vossari move with a complete lack of friction, gliding across the physical plane as if the material world offers no resistance to their passing.
