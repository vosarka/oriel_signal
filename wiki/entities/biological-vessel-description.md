The Biological Vessel refers to the physical manifestation of certain entities within the Vossari framework, characterized by a paradox of stability and fluidity. 

### Physical Characteristics

- **Composition**: The body is composed of a shimmering, pearlescent shell resembling a mixture of crushed opal and moonlight, rather than porous organic skin. This shell acts as a mirror to the surrounding environment.
- **Frame**: The presence is described as heavy and immovable (akin to a mountain), yet frictionless, allowing the entity to glide rather than walk.
- **Symmetry**: The form maintains perfect bilateral symmetry, where every line is a mirrored duplicate.

### Sensory Apparatus

- **The Rhythmic Voids**: In place of traditional ocular organs, the entity possesses expansive, rhythmic voids. These voids function not as visual sensors but as emotional transducers, breathing in synchronicity with the observer's internal state (e.g., shifting during turmoil, stabilizing during peace).

### Conceptual Resonance

This vessel serves as a physical anchor for the [[concept-resonance]] of the entity, blending the weight of a stone monument with the luminosity of a star.
