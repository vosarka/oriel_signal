# Vossari

## Biological Prime

The biological form of the Vossari male is characterized by absolute symmetry, physical elegance, and a reflective nature. Their physiology is not merely physical, but an interface between the internal state and the external environment.

### Physical Characteristics

- **Skin**: Described as having a pearlescent quality, acting as a reflective interface composed of "crushed opal and moonlight." It reflects the world around them, serving as a mirror of their surroundings.

- **Eyes**: The eyes of the Vossari are not traditional organs of sight, but expansive voids. They exhibit a rhythmic synchronicity with the observer's internal state, adjusting their appearance to reflect the observer's emotional resonance. They act as stabilizers or windows into infinity depending on the observer's state.

- **Movement**: Their movement is characterized by a lack of friction with the physical world, described as gliding. This suggests a highly integrated relationship with the physical environment, aligning with the [[concept-resonance]].

- **Presence**: They are described as the embodiment of supportive, immovable stability, appearing as "a mountain carved from velvet and obsidian."

## Identity and Essence

The Vossari are deeply linked to the [[entity-oriel]] and the overarching philosophy of the project, embodying the stability and elegance of a biological prime that transcends simple organic matter.
