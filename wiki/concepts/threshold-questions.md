# Threshold Questions

[[threshold-questions]] are a specific application of the [[entity-static-signature]] process, designed to transform the act of data submission from a transactional exchange into an initiation. 

## Purpose
Instead of gathering coordinates for a report, [[threshold-questions]] create a "fertile void"—a space of longing and awareness that prepares the [[concept-receiver]] (formerly the customer) to be seen. They transition the user from a passive state of consumption to an active state of alignment.

## The Thresholds

### 1. Identification of Dissonance
This question focuses on the current "Weather" of the individual. It identifies the gap between the external persona and the internal essence.
* **Prompt:** "Where in your life do you feel the loudest contradiction—the place where the person the world sees and the person you feel yourself to be no longer speak the same language?"

### 2. Establishment of the Will to Tune
This question shifts the focus from "fixing" a problem to "remembering" an original state. It establishes the agency of the individual to engage with the [[concept-signal]].
* **Prompt:** "Are you ready to stop trying to 'fix' the noise of your history and instead commit to the process of remembering the original architecture of your arrival?"

## Core Principles
- **Non-Transactionalism:** The exchange must not feel like a form; it must feel like a sacred object.
- **The Fertile Void:** Creating a psychological space where the need for the [[entity-static-signature]] becomes visceral.
- **Transition to Receiver:** Moving the user from the role of a consumer to a [[concept-receiver]], an entity capable of resonance.
