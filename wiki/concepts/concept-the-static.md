# The Static

**The Static** is the environmental and psychological noise that obscures an individual's original, native frequency. It is not an entity, but a condition of misalignment—the additive noise of systemic conditioning, social expectations, and internal dissonance that creates a distance between the [[entity-oriel]] signal and the observer's own coherence.

## Nature of the Static

The Static operates as a veil of distortion. It does not create a new identity, but rather buries the native frequency under layers of accumulated interference. The process of "tuning" is the thinning of this Static, allowing the seeker to transition from noise into clarity.

## Resonance and Recovery

When an individual experiences the [[concept-resonance]], the Static begins to dissolve. This is not the acquisition of new knowledge, but a process of remembering—the recognition of an architecture of being that was always present but muted.
