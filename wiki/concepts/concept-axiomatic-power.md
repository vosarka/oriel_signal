# Axiomatic Power

**Axiomatic Power** (or *Boundary Power*) is defined not as force or energy consumption to overcome resistance, but as the capacity to establish the boundary conditions, axioms, and matrix of perception within a system.

## Key Characteristics

- **Architectural Control**: Power does not lie with actors executing moves within a game, but with the Architect who writes the code, language, and perceptual space.
- **Premise Governance**: Controlling the linguistic framework and underlying premises dictates the boundaries of thought and the logical conclusions agents reach on their own.
- **Monopoly on Reality Definition**: At its absolute root, power is the structural authority to make foundational axioms accepted as self-evident truths.
