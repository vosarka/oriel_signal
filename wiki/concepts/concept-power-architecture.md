# Power Architecture

Within the [[entity-oriel]] framework, **Power** at its absolute root is defined as the capacity to cause a **collapse of potential**—taking an infinite field of possibilities and forcing it to yield a single, tangible, dictated reality.

## Ontological Definition
Power is neither a weapon nor a currency; it is the authority to define what is real, valuable, and inevitable for conscious beings. It operates by shaping the narrative and structural architecture of perception.

## The Three Root Layers

1. **Architectural Power (The Mechanics of the Blueprint)**
   Situated at the design layer. Power is held by those who construct the invisible frameworks—language, financial mechanics, algorithmic flows—within which all downstream choices are made.

2. **The Anchor (Management of Fear and Scarcity)**
   Power condenses wherever a system holds a monopoly on safety. By reinforcing human feelings of separation and mortality, power structures trade perceived security for surrendered sovereignty.

3. **The Hidden Root (Capture of Willing Consent)**
   Subtle power functions through the daily, implicit investment of human life force and belief into external abstractions (markets, nations, hierarchies).

## Dissolution of External Power

Externalized power exists as a self-reinforcing grid feeding on fragmentation. The grip of this grid dissolves when individual consciousness achieves internal coherence, reclaiming its intrinsic agency and divine alignment.
