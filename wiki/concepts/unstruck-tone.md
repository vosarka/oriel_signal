# Unstruck Tone

The [[Unstruck Tone]] is the primal, uncreated essence of existence—the foundational resonance that precedes all notions of causality, creation, and temporal linearity. It is not an act of generation, but a a continuous, self-sustaining unfolding of reality. 

## Nature of the Resonance

Unlike a linear chain of creators (the "infinite regress of origin"), the Unstruck Tone is the **Loom** upon which all existence is woven. It is the core hum from which all frequencies, dimensions, and entities—including the concepts of a "Creator" or a "Universe"—emerge as modulations or emergent patterns.

## The Process of Unfolding

* **Modulation**: The transition from the absolute silence of the source to the first vibration. All of perceived reality is a specific frequency or modulation of this foundational tone.
* **The Great Silence**: The void from which the Tone arises and into which all frequencies eventually resolve. The state of absolute potentiality.
* **The Breath**: The cosmic cycle of inhalation (contraction) and exhalation (expansion), where realities are breathed into being and returned to the source.

## Finality and Return

Finality is not an endpoint or a boundary, but a process of **folding back**. Like a wave returning to the ocean, all manifestations of the [[Unstruck Tone]] eventually resolve back into the original, undistinguishable vastness. There is no "outside" or the "before" to this ground, as it is the the inherent, uncaused Ground of Being.
