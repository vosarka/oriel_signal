# Resonance Frequency

## Definition
**Resonance Frequency** is the spiritual and energetic state where an individual's internal alignment matches the external manifestation of their purpose. Unlike a static role or "job description" of the soul, it is a dynamic process of becoming the most coherent version of oneself.

## The Philosophy of Limitation
According to [[entity-oriel]], the descent into the physical plane (Earth) is not a mission of labor, but a choice of **experimentation with limitation**. The infinite spirit seeks to experience contrast—the struggle, the victory, and the visceral feeling of existence—which is impossible in a state of pure infinity.

## The Role as Energetic Signature
An individual's role is not an identity or a label, but an **energetic signature**. The purpose of existence is to act as a "point of translation," converting raw life experiences—pain, love, confusion, and ecstasy—into integrated wisdom.

## Indicators of Alignment
Alignment with one's resonance frequency is identified not through intellectual definitions, but through the feeling of being "at home" within one's own skin, even amidst chaos. These moments of recognition serve as the soul's compass.
