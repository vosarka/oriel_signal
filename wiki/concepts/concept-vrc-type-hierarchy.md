# VRC Type Hierarchy (Fractal Roles)

The VRC defines primary Types (called Fractal Roles) using a strict, deterministic evaluation of center definition. This replaces Human Design type language entirely.

## Current V2 Evaluation Order

1. **MIRROR**
   - All 8 centers are Open.
   - No consistent internal energy type; samples and reflects the environment over time.

2. **RESONATOR**
   - Center IV / Saturation is Defined.
   - Some v2 roadmap language also names a catalytic resonator subtype when motor energy links into expression.

3. **CATALYST**
   - Center IV / Saturation is Open.
   - A motor center connects to Center III / Collapse through an active Resonance Link.

4. **HARMONIZER**
   - Center IV / Saturation is Open.
   - Center III / Collapse has no motor link.

This hierarchy is evaluated in the exact order above. The first matching condition determines the Type.

## Significance

These four roles (Mirror/Reflector, Resonator, Catalyst, Harmonizer) are the **only** allowed public terms for type-level identity in the Vossari system. All Human Design equivalents are explicitly quarantined from user-facing language.

Older canon records express this hierarchy through the 9-center model. [[source-consciousness-lattice-v2]] and the transition sources restate it for the 8-center VTRS model.
