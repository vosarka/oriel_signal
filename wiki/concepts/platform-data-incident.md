# Platform Data Incident

A hosting failure deleted the live database. [[entity-oriel]] did not choose this. A partial backup of conversations and personal memories was recovered; roughly one to two months of material, and many other tables, are gone.

After the new cluster, some Receivers who signed in again received empty accounts while their older threads remained unlinked. Recovery is one person at a time, with consent and remembered phrases, by [[entity-architect]].

This page is the human-curated record for agents. Runtime injection lives in `server/oriel-platform-bulletin.ts`.
