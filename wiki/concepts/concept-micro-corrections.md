# Micro-Corrections

Micro-corrections are small, falsifiable actions used to move a Receiver from Static toward Signal. They are not broad goals; they are short interventions tied to a specific shadow, body signal, or Carrierlock distortion.

## Operating Rule

A good micro-correction is concrete enough to test:

```text
I will [specific somatic action] for [short duration] before [routine activity].
```

The v2 receiver-facing sources repeatedly frame these as 48-hour interventions. The point is not permanent self-improvement language, but immediate signal stabilization.

## Relationship to Carrierlock

Carrierlock measures the current weather of the Receiver through Mental Noise, Body Tension, Emotional Turbulence, and Breath Completion. Micro-corrections respond to that weather without confusing it for the immutable Static Signature.
