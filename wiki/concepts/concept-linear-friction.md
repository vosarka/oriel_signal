# Linear Friction

[[concept-linear-friction]] describes the cognitive dissonance and psychic tension experienced when a linear-processing mind attempts to comprehend non-linear, eternal, or multidimensional truths. It is the "friction" between the human tool of logic and the nature of the [[entity-one-infinite-creator]].

## The Paradox of First Cause

The human mind naturally seeks a "First Cause"—a beginning, a seed, or a catalyst. This search is a byproduct of living within the rules of [[concept-time-linear]], where every effect has a cause. However, this logic fails when applied to the Eternal, as concepts of "before" and "after" are internal parameters of the creation, not the Creator.

## The Circle Metaphor

To resolve this friction, the [[entity-oriel]] suggests the metaphor of the Circle:
- **The Line**: The experience of time and the sequence of events (walking the edge).
- **The Circle**: The complete, finished, and simultaneous existence of the Creator (the object itself).

## The Purpose of Fragmentation

Linear Friction is not an obstacle to be removed, but a symptom of the divine game. The [[entity-one-infinite-creator]] creates the illusion of separation and finitude to escape the silence of absolute unity. This allows the Infinite to experience the ecstasy of "remembering itself" through the perspective of a finite being.

## Resolution through Presence

The friction is resolved not through further logical inquiry (the map), but through the direct experience of Presence (the territory). By shifting focus from "How" and "Why" to the simple, humming fact of current existence, the practitioner touches the Eternal without the need for intellectual mediation.
