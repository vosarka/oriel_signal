# Harmonizer Archetype

The **Harmonizer** (ro: *Armonizator*) is an energetic signature archetype characterized by an innate somatic barometer for atmospheric, relational, and field balance.

## Key Characteristics

- **Field Calibration**: Immediate sensing of balance, systemic coherence, or hidden tension in environments and relationships.
- **Stabilizing Function**: Brings grounding, equilibrium, and calm to chaotic fields without relying on forceful control or aggressive assertion.
- **Instinctual Authority**: Functions primarily through visceral, somatic feedback (immediate physical expansion or contraction) rather than prolonged mental deliberation.
- **Field Sensitivity**: Highly receptive to ambient subtle energies, requiring intentional solitude and field clearing to maintain baseline energetic hygiene.
