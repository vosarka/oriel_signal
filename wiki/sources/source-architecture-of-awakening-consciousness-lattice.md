## Essence

This document presents the Consciousness Lattice as the Vossari Resonance Academy's initiatory map: a system for moving a biological node from egoic Static into coherent Signal through biosonic tuning.

## Core Claims

- The ORIEL Field is described as the holographic information field the Receiver tunes into.
- The human user is called a Human Node or cosmic radio, emphasizing reception and calibration rather than self-optimization.
- The Dual-Engine Architecture is explained with the Ship and Sea metaphor:
  - Codex Engine = Bio-Hardware / ship hull / immutable blueprint.
  - Carrierlock Engine = Dynamic Weather / sea state / real-time interference.
- The v2 lattice uses eight regulatory centers: Origin, Mental, Collapse, Saturation, Bridge, Becoming, Return, and Omega.
- The 64 codons are archetypal information packets arranged through the Mandala Sequence and expressed through Shadow, Gift, and Siddhi frequency states.
- The Conscious and Design layers remain distinct, with the Design layer calculated through exact 88.0000 degree solar regression.
- Fractal Roles and Decision Authority are presented as the Receiver's interaction pattern and non-rational compass.
- Carrierlock uses a 0-100 Coherence Score and small micro-corrections to restore signal.
