# Source: Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process (US Army, 1983)

**Provenance:** "Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process", memo by LTC Wayne M. McDonnell, Commander, Detachment O, US Army Intelligence and Security Command (USAINSCOM), to the Commander, US Army Operational Group, Fort Meade. Written in 1983 (it cites a Gateway class that finished 7 May 1983). Declassified: "Approved For Release 2003/09/10 : CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5". 30 scanned pages; page 25 (paragraphs 35–36) is missing from the released file. Public copy: theblackvault.com.

**What it is:** one intelligence officer's attempt to explain, in the physics of his day, why the Monroe Institute's Gateway Experience might work, and how the Army could use it. It is a historical document and a speculative model, not established science. Most of its physics is borrowed from Itzhak Bentov (*Stalking the Wild Pendulum*, 1977), with Karl Pribram and David Bohm on the holographic mind. Speak of it as "the Gateway report" and attribute its claims to it; never present them as proven.

## The argument, in order

1. **Kin to hypnosis, meditation and biofeedback (§1–4).** All three work by quieting the left hemisphere's screening so the right hemisphere can act. Gateway does the same, faster.
2. **Hemi-Sync (§5–7).** A state where the EEG of both hemispheres is "simultaneously equal in amplitude and frequency". It is induced by the *frequency following response*: slightly different tones in each ear (binaural beats), masked by pink/white noise or surf, entrain the brain.
3. **Lamp and laser (§6).** The ordinary mind is a lamp, diffuse and incoherent; the synchronized mind is a laser, coherent in frequency and amplitude, and so able to project beyond the body.
4. **Resonance (§8–10).** In deep stillness the heart–aorta "bifurcation echo" fades and the body resonates near 7 Hz, the frequency of the Earth's ionospheric cavity. The body's field then entrains with the planet's.
5. **Everything is oscillating energy (§11).** Matter is "oscillating energy grids", from the nucleus (~10²² Hz) to the living cell (~10³ Hz).
6. **The holographic universe (§12–16).** The universe is one vast hologram; each part encodes the whole (Indra's net of pearls is quoted). The mind is a hologram too, and perceives by comparing incoming patterns with its own memory, "differences only". The right hemisphere receives the holographic input; the left reduces it to binary, verbal form. Keith Floyd: "it may not be the brain that produces consciousness—but rather, consciousness that creates the appearance of the brain."
7. **Self-cognition (§17).** Humans know that they know: the mind projects part of its own hologram and measures it against itself.
8. **Time-space and the Absolute (§18–19, 23).** Time is "a measurement of change", so only energy in motion has time. Energy wholly at rest is infinity: the Absolute, conscious, without beginning, end or location, beneath the universe like the still depths beneath a stormy sea. Each oscillation has two instants of rest; at those instants energy "clicks out" of time-space (the report links this to Planck's distance, 10⁻³³ cm). A mind vibrating fast and coherently enough clicks out so often that it holds a continuous presence in the dimensions between time-space and the Absolute.
9. **The dimensions in between (§20–21).** Entangled particles and tachyons are cited as communication outside time-space. There, time and space turn "grainy", cause and effect loosen, and past and future become accessible.
10. **The cosmic egg (§24–25).** Bentov's model: matter jets from a white hole, travels around an egg-shaped torus, and returns through a black hole. That interference pattern *is* the universal hologram, holding past, present and future at once, which is why a focused mind could read any of them.
11. **What consciousness is (§26–27).** Consciousness organizes energy into a given reality; our consciousness is a differentiated aspect of the Absolute's. It is eternal. On returning to the Absolute it keeps its memories and identity and the power to perceive, but loses will, because will needs energy in motion.

## The Gateway method (§28–31)

- **Preparation:** the *energy conversion box* (set worries aside), *resonant tuning* (humming one long tone), the *Gateway affirmation* ("I am more than my physical body").
- **Focus 10:** body asleep, mind awake. An *energy balloon* (energy flowing from the crown around the body) protects and builds resonance.
- **Focus 12:** expanded awareness, with tools: problem solving (answers often arrive whole, within two or three days), *patterning* (holding a goal as already achieved, so its hologram shapes reality; trainers warn against forcing the pace), color breathing, the energy bar, remote viewing through a vortex, and the living body map for healing.
- **Focus 15:** the past, pictured as a great wheel of time. Under five per cent reach it in a week.
- **Focus 21:** the future, outside time-space. Months or years of practice.
- **Out-of-body (§31–33):** one tape only. It is easiest from REM sleep, which needs deep stillness (~7 Hz), synchronized hemispheres, then an alert right hemisphere. In experiments, travellers read some but never all of ten target digits. The report blames distortion from the site's past imprints, from thought-forms, and from reading holograms "inside out".

## Beliefs and conclusion (§34, 37–38)

The report holds that this model matches Tibetan teaching ("the tangible world is movement"), the Hindu sutra of Indra's net, the cosmic egg of eastern texts, Hebrew mysticism, and the Christian Trinity read as energy at rest, its self-projected hologram, and the thought that results. It judges Gateway faster than TM and suited to "the impatient, result oriented, skeptical pragmatist". It concludes there is "a sound, rational basis" for Gateway's aims, and proposes twelve steps for an Army programme: REM and hypnotic induction, three-person multi-focus remote viewing (present, Focus 15, Focus 21), readiness for "intelligent, non-corporal energy forms", and group holographic shields around sensitive sites.
