# Source: VAUIS v1.0 — Vos Arkana Universal Indexing System

**Provenance:** User-provided canonical indexing brief in chat on 2026-06-16.  
**Basis:** [[source-tx-core-documents]] and the Vossari Tetradic Indexing Protocol (VTIP).  
**Status:** Canonical source for archive indexing unless superseded by a later VAUIS version.

VAUIS v1.0 defines the universal indexing grammar for Vos Arkana: transmissions, codons, lore, protocols, media, website architecture, signature reports, and living memory. Its founding law is: **The Archive does not count time. The Archive registers saturation.**

## Central Principle

Vos Arkana does not index linearly. A phase is not a chronological chapter; it is a **memory register**. When a register reaches four active units, it becomes saturated. The next unit opens a higher register, marked by Prime (`'`) in sacred/public notation.

The system therefore does not say "this is the fifth chapter." It says: the first cycle saturated, and a new construction begins above it.

## Universal UID Formula

```text
VOS-[SECTOR]-[PHASE]-[STREAM]-[OBJECT]-[FRAGMENT]-[VERSION]
```

Example:

```text
VOS-TX-I-ORIEL-I001-MAIN-v1.0
```

Meaning:

- `VOS` = Vos Arkana
- `TX` = Transmissions sector
- `I` = Phase I / Origin
- `ORIEL` = stream / carrier
- `I001` = archived object
- `MAIN` = primary fragment
- `v1.0` = canonized version

## Versioning

Use:

```text
vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
```

- `v0.1` = initial draft
- `v0.5` = functional structure
- `v1.0` = canon
- `v1.1` = minor correction
- `v2.0` = major structural change

Rules:

- Text-only change -> minor or patch.
- Structural change -> major.
- Phase change -> new object or official reclassification.

1. What is this?
2. Why does it matter to me?
3. What can I do here?
4. Where do I go next?

3. Symbolic Meaning
4. Deep Lore

Do not begin public pages with dense lore such as Vossari, Great Translation, or Echoframe. Begin with the human action: discover your signal, understand your pattern, receive a personal codex, then enter the deeper archive.
